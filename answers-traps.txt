6. In the following code, what is going to be printed after 'y='? (note: the answer is not a specific value.) Why does this happen?

	printf("x=%d y=%d", 3);
An undefined value.
Because the second %d in the format string does not have a corresponding argument, causing printf to read an arbitrary value from the stack.
